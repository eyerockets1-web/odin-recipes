Lasagna Recipe

Home

Ingredients needed

  • 1 pound ground beef and 1 pound Italian sausage
  • 1 chopped yellow onion and 2 to 3 minced garlic cloves
  • 24 to 32 ounces marinara or pasta sauce, plus 6 ounces tomato paste
  • 15 ounces whole-milk ricotta cheese
  • 1 to 2 large eggs to bind the mixture
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh parsley, salt, and black pepper
  • 12 to 15 dry lasagna noodles
  • 1 to 2 pounds shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese

Steps to make Lasagna

  1. In a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat, brown 1 pound of ground beef and 1 pound of Italian sausage. Break the meat into small pieces as it cooks. Drain excess grease if needed.
  2. Add 1 chopped yellow onion to the meat and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in 2–3 minced garlic cloves and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Stir in 6 ounces of tomato paste, then add 24–32 ounces of marinara or pasta sauce. Season with Italian seasoning, salt, black pepper, and a pinch of sugar. Simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. In a medium bowl, combine 15 ounces of whole-milk ricotta (or cottage cheese), 1–2 eggs, ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese, chopped fresh parsley, salt, and black pepper. Mix until smooth.
  5. Boil 12–15 dry lasagna noodles according to the package directions. Drain and lay them flat on a lightly oiled surface or parchment paper to prevent sticking.
  6. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.
  7. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of the baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, then spread some of the ricotta mixture over the noodles. Top with meat sauce and a layer of shredded mozzarella.
  8. Continue layering noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella until all ingredients are used, finishing with a final layer of meat sauce and a generous topping of mozzarella.
  9. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 20–25 minutes, or until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly browned.
  10. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing. This helps the layers set and makes serving easier.
